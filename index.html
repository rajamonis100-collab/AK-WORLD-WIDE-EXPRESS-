Terms of Carriage & Shipping Policies

Standard Operating Guidelines

1. Customs & Duties Policy

All international shipments are subject to destination customs inspection. Import duties, VAT, and local regulatory taxes are payable by the consignee unless agreed otherwise.

2. Prohibited & Restricted Items

Dangerous goods (IATA restricted), hazardous chemicals, currency, narcotics, firearms, and counterfeit merchandise are strictly prohibited from air carriage.

3. Volumetric Weight Calculation

Freight charges apply on actual gross weight or volumetric weight (L x W x H in cm / 5000), whichever is higher, in accordance with international aviation standards.

4. Free Pickup & Claims Policy

Complimentary doorstep collection is provided in active service zones. Any transit damage or shortage claims must be reported in writing within 7 days of delivery.
